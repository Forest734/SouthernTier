# Changelog

Notable changes to the Southern Tier web app. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the project uses
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The QGIS map as a web app: open space, trails, trail routes, the counties, and Connecticut
  Hill's roads and old roads, over NYS imagery, with 1 m lidar elevation, hillshade and land use
  to switch on. Colours, labels and the zooms they show at follow the QGIS project.
- Click or tap for what's there: every layer under the point, as QGIS's map tips.
- Search by name for parks, forests, trails, routes and the old roads, with the park or forest
  each trail is in.
- Go to the whole map, a county, or Connecticut Hill. The nine old roads, with their notes.
- Layer choices and the last view are kept; the URL follows the view.
- My location, and metric and imperial scale bars.
- Installable to the home screen, and works offline once opened (but for the imagery and
  hillshade). Light and dark panel; a drawer on phones.
